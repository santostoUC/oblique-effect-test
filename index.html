Oblique Effect: Visual Acuity Experiment

A short classroom demonstration of orientation-dependent visual resolution.

Before you begin

Use the device you will use for the experiment. Hold it at a comfortable, consistent viewing distance. Do not zoom the browser.

Important: This is a classroom demonstration, not a clinical vision test. Screen size, viewing distance, and display quality affect the result.

Watch the lines

The lines will become progressively finer. Press the button as soon as you can no longer clearly resolve the individual lines.

You can also press the SPACE bar.
